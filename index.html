HOW TO PLAY
KEYBOARD

Space / Up = jump, hold it for a higher jump

Down = duck, in the air = fall fast

P / Esc = pause · M = sound

TOUCH

Tap = jump, keep the finger down for a higher jump

Swipe down = duck (keep the finger down to stay ducked), in the air = fall fast

Pause button in the top corner

Combo: jump the prop, then Down / swipe down to drop under the beehive branch.
